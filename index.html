ExplainAlert: Explainable Anomaly Detection for Cloud Servers

M.Tech Research Project | June 2026 | Guide: Dr. Srinivas A H
  • Detection: Isolation Forest on AWS EC2, reaching 97.9% accuracy and 100% recall on 239 labeled CloudWatch samples (CPU, network, disk) collected over 20 hours, with anomalies injected using Linux stress tools.
